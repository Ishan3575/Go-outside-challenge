AI Verification & Privacy

Documented system design & open architecture

1. Multimodal Vision Model

Photo evidence is evaluated on the server side using Google Gemini 3.8 Flash. The model assesses whether the visual features in the photograph correspond to the claimed planting, cleanup, nature walk, or exploration challenge.

2. Evidence Assessment, Not Absolute Proof

Photographs alone cannot reliably prove the exact capture timestamp or physical coordinates. The AI output is treated as a structured relevance assessment rather than unquestionable proof. Ambiguous or unverified submissions remain pending community review.

3. Privacy-First Geolocation

Users may choose browser geolocation or manual selection. Exact residential coordinates are never logged or exposed. Leaderboards display general regional areas (e.g., "Metro Bay Area") rather than precise user locations.

4. Server-Side Security

All API keys and scoring algorithms reside exclusively on the server. Scoring rules and bonus multipliers are calculated server-side based on actual submission timestamps.
